Connect Custom Domain Free SSL 🔒

Map your personal domain or subdomain to your live portfolio.

Required DNS Record
1
Add DNS Record: Go to your domain registrar (cPanel, Cloudflare, GoDaddy, Namecheap) and add the CNAME record shown above.
2
Verify in DreemFolio: Enter your domain above and click Verify & Connect.
3
Publish Live: Click "Publish Live Portfolio" in the editor. Our automated engine issues your free SSL certificate within 30-60 seconds!
cPanel: Go to Zone Editor → Click Manage on your domain → Click Add Record → Type: CNAME → Name: subdomain → Record: cname.dreemfolio.com.
Cloudflare: Go to DNS Records → Add Record → Type: CNAME → Name: subdomain → Target: cname.dreemfolio.com → Set Proxy to DNS only (Grey Cloud ⚪).
GoDaddy / Namecheap: Open DNS Management / Advanced DNS → Add New Record → Type: CNAME → Host: subdomain → Target/Value: cname.dreemfolio.com.
