Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)

Create an ATS-Friendly Resume

Many employers use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to organize, scan, and filter incoming job applications. A clear resume structure, standard section titles, and job-specific keywords make your resume easier for these systems and human recruiters to process.

DreemFolio helps you review your resume against target requirements and identify areas where relevant skills, certifications, and responsibilities may be missing.

Standard Single-column layout easily parsed by Workday, Greenhouse & Lever
100% Text Selectable, vector font layers without rasterized image clipping

What ATS Parsers Look For:

  • Recognized Section Headings: Standard names such as "Work Experience", "Education", and "Skills" rather than creative labels.
  • Chronological Work History: Explicit job titles, company names, and date ranges (Month/Year) that parsers can index.
  • Hard & Soft Skill Match: Directly aligning technical languages, tools, and credentials with the employer's posting.
  • Clean Typography: Universal fonts with zero complex floating graphic layers or tables.
