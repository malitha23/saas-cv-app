We are dedicated to providing fair, transparent, and trustworthy services. This policy explains our digital fulfillment rules, refund eligibility, and how to request a reversal via the PayHere Payment Gateway.

1 Digital Service Fulfillment & Delivery Policy

DreemFolio AI is an entirely cloud-hosted Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) application providing digital resume optimization, ATS scoring, and career document generation.

Instant Electronic Delivery Guarantee (PayHere Verified)

- Zero Physical Dispatch: We do not manufacture or ship physical goods. No postal delivery or logistics handling fees are charged.
- Instant Provisioning: As soon as your transaction is confirmed via the PayHere Payment Gateway (or once your bank deposit slip is approved), your subscription privileges and watermark-free download capabilities are activated immediately in real time.
- Digital Invoicing: An electronic receipt containing your unique Order ID, date, currency (LKR / USD), and active duration is immediately generated and dispatched to your registered email address.

2 Refund Eligibility Criteria

In accordance with PayHere merchant guidelines and consumer fair-trading practices, refund requests are evaluated according to the following transparent criteria:

Circumstances Eligible for 100% Full Refund:
  • Cooling-off Period: You submitted a refund request within 7 to 14 calendar days of purchase and have not generated or downloaded more than 2 premium resumes during the active period.
  • Verified Technical Failure: You encountered an unresolvable technical fault (such as continuous PDF compilation failure or platform unavailability) that our technical team was unable to rectify within 24 hours.
  • Duplicate Transaction: You were inadvertently charged multiple times for the same plan due to a payment gateway latency or browser timeout.
  • Non-Activation: Your payment was debited by PayHere, but system quotas failed to provision automatically and you reported the incident before consuming services.
Circumstances Ineligible for Refund:
  • Requests submitted more than 14 calendar days after the transaction date.
  • Accounts that have actively generated, customized, and downloaded 3 or more watermark-free ATS resumes or visual CVs (meaning the digital service has been substantially consumed).
  • Dissatisfaction arising from third-party recruitment outcomes (such as not receiving a job interview, as DreemFolio AI cannot guarantee employer hiring decisions).
  • Accounts suspended or permanently banned for violating our Acceptable Use Policy (e.g., fraudulent credentials, automated scraping, or security tampering).

4 How to Submit a Refund Request

Users can request a refund through either of the following official channels:

Option 1: In-App Refund Portal (Fastest)

Log in and navigate to My Profile & Billing (/profile). Scroll to your Payments & Subscriptions History, locate your order, and click the Request Refund button. Enter your reason and submit.

Option 2: Direct Email Desk

Send an email directly to billing@dreemfolio.com or support@dreemfolio.com with your registered email, Order Reference ID, and a brief description of the issue.

5 Refund Processing Timeline & Gateway Reversals

- Review & Approval: Refund requests are reviewed and decided by our billing team within 24 to 48 hours of submission.

- Gateway Reversal: Once approved, the refund is initiated programmatically through the PayHere Refund API back to the exact credit card, debit card, or payment method originally utilized.

- Bank Reflection Timeframe: Under standard banking protocols in Sri Lanka and internationally, funds typically reflect in the customer's bank statement within 3 to 7 business days (or up to 10 working days depending on the customer's card-issuing bank).

- Original Currency: Refunds are processed in the exact currency originally billed (LKR or USD).
