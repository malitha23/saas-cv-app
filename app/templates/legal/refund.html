Refund & Cancellation Policy

We believe in 100% transparent pricing and honest customer service. Here is how subscription cancellations and refunds work at ResuMatch AI.

14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

Full refund available within 14 days of purchase if you have not heavily consumed premium downloads.

No Hidden Traps

Cancel your recurring subscription with one click. No mandatory calls, surveys, or retention gymnastics.

Technical Glitch Protection

If our AI or PDF rendering fails to generate your documents properly, you receive an immediate full refund.

1 General Overview

ResuMatch AI provides instant access to digital software, AI generative compute, and downloadable PDF assets. Because digital software and compute power are consumed upon execution, our refund policy balances consumer protection with the reality of non-tangible digital deliverables.

2 14-Day Refund Eligibility Criteria

In accordance with international consumer protection directives (including EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU), users may request a full refund within 14 calendar days of their initial subscription charge under the following circumstances:

Eligible for 100% Full Refund:
  • You purchased a recurring subscription (Pro Career or Executive Elite) and have not generated or downloaded more than 2 premium resumes during the billing period.
  • You experienced a verified technical error (e.g., PDF generation failed, system outage) that our technical support team could not resolve within 24 hours.
  • You were accidentally charged duplicate transactions due to a payment gateway timeout.
Ineligible for Refund:
  • More than 14 calendar days have elapsed since the transaction date.
  • The user has actively downloaded numerous tailored resumes and visual CVs, thereby fully consuming the digital service.
  • 7-Day Sprint Passes ($4.99 / Rs. 490) once activated and utilized to download watermark-free documents.
  • Accounts suspended or terminated due to violations of our Acceptable Use Policy (e.g., fraudulent resumes, scraping).

3 How to Cancel Your Subscription

You can cancel your subscription at any time with immediate effect:

  1. Log in to your ResuMatch AI account.
  2. Click on your Profile Icon or Plan Badge in the top-right header.
  3. Select Manage Subscription Plans from the dropdown menu.
  4. Click Cancel Subscription or submit a one-click cancellation request.

Alternatively, email us at billing@resumatch.ai with your account email address, and our support team will cancel your renewal within 24 hours.

Access Retention: When you cancel, you will not be billed again. Your account will retain all Pro/Elite features until the end of your prepaid billing period. Afterwards, your account gracefully downgrades to the Free Starter tier with all saved resumes intact.

4 How to Request a Refund

To submit a refund request, send an email to billing@resumatch.ai containing:

  • Your account registered email address.
  • Transaction ID or date of payment.
  • A brief sentence describing your reason (e.g. accidental purchase, unexpected charge, or technical issue).

Approved refunds are processed immediately back to the original payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, or local payment provider). Funds typically reflect on your bank statement within 3 to 7 business days depending on your financial institution.

5 Chargebacks & Dispute Resolution

We encourage you to contact us directly before initiating a bank chargeback. In the overwhelming majority of cases, we can issue an instant refund directly, avoiding bank dispute fees and potential account freezes.
