Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Your career data belongs strictly to you. This policy outlines how DreemFolio AI collects, processes, and protects your personal information in full compliance with the Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 (PDPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and international payment security frameworks.

Zero Payment Card Storage

Card details are handled exclusively by PayHere (PCI-DSS Level 1). We never store or see your raw card numbers or CVV.

Zero AI Training Retention

Your uploaded resumes, career records, and target job descriptions are never used to train foundation AI models.

We Never Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal career information or contact lists to recruiters, data brokers, or advertisers.

1 Introduction & Data Controller

DreemFolio AI ("DreemFolio", "we", "our", or "us") operates the SaaS platform located at dreemfolio.com, providing automated resume optimization, ATS scoring, and portfolio publishing.

Under the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 of Sri Lanka (PDPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), DreemFolio AI acts as the Data Controller for your account credentials, billing records, and authentication data, and as a Data Processor for user-submitted resume files and cover letters.

Data Protection Officer (DPO): Inquiries regarding your personal data rights may be submitted directly to our DPO at privacy@dreemfolio.com (Hambanthota, Sri Lanka).

2 Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: Full name, registered email address, and one-way cryptographic password hashes (salted using bcrypt).
  • Google OAuth 2.0 Information: When signing in with Google, we receive your Google ID, verified email address, full name, and avatar image URL via standard scopes (openid, email, profile).
  • Resume & Career Documents: Uploaded CV documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT), employment history, education, certifications, projects, and target job postings pasted for keyword scoring.
  • Transaction & Billing Metadata: Order reference ID, payment date, plan duration (1M, 3M, 6M, 12M, Lifetime), currency (LKR or USD), payment method used (e.g. Visa/MasterCard, Frimi, eZ Cash, Bank Transfer), and subscription expiry date. We do not collect or store card numbers.
  • Technical Device Data: IP address, browser user-agent, operating system, and geolocation (country-level) used solely to apply Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) pricing and mitigate fraud.

3 Payment Gateway Security & Non-Storage of Financial Data

All financial transactions for subscriptions and passes are processed securely through PayHere (Private) Limited, an authorized Payment Service Provider regulated by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka:

Strict Zero Card Data Storage Policy:

DreemFolio AI never collects, intercepts, views, or stores your credit card numbers, debit card details, CVV/CVC codes, card expiration dates, or bank passwords. All card details are transmitted directly from your web browser to PayHere's 256-bit encrypted checkout servers. PayHere is fully certified under the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI-DSS Level 1).

Payment Tokens & Notifications (IPN):

Upon successful payment, PayHere transmits an encrypted, digitally signed Instant Payment Notification (IPN) with a hash signature verifying that the payment was completed. DreemFolio AI saves only the transaction reference ID, payment status, and order total to activate your subscription quota.

4 Sri Lanka PDPA Compliance & User Privacy Rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 (PDPA) of Sri Lanka, GDPR, and CCPA, every registered user is entitled to the following statutory rights:

Right of Access (Section 13 PDPA):

You can request confirmation of whether we process your data and receive an export of all resumes and account information.

Right to Rectification (Section 14 PDPA):

You may modify, correct, or update incomplete or inaccurate career information directly via your dashboard at any time.

Right to Erasure / Be Forgotten (Section 15 PDPA):

You can delete saved cloud resumes or submit an account deletion request to erase your data permanently.

Right to Withdraw Consent:

You may withdraw your consent for processing at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

5 Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

DreemFolio AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We only access Google data to authenticate your identity and display your profile picture.
  • We never sell, rent, or transfer your Google profile data to data brokers or advertising networks.
  • We never use Google profile data to train generalized AI/ML models.

6 Generative AI Processing & Zero Training Retention

DreemFolio AI connects to enterprise generative AI APIs (such as Google Gemini) to tailor resume content and calculate ATS keyword metrics.

Strict Enterprise Privacy Guarantees

All data submitted to generative inference engines is transmitted over TLS 1.3 encrypted tunnels and processed ephemerally. Enterprise parameters are enforced so that your resume content, career history, and job descriptions are NEVER retained or utilized to train or fine-tune public foundation AI models.

7 Data Security & Retention Policies

- Data in Transit: All HTTP communications are strictly encrypted using TLS 1.3 with HTTPS enforcement and HSTS security headers.
- Data at Rest: Cloud resumes and candidate records are stored in secure relational databases with isolated tenant schemas and strict row-level access controls.
- File Uploads: Uploaded resume files (PDF, DOCX) are parsed entirely in ephemeral RAM buffers and purged immediately following structured data extraction.
- Retention Period: Resumes and account data remain saved until you delete them or close your account. Transaction history is retained for statutory accounting periods mandated by Sri Lankan tax regulations.

8 Cookies & Local Browser Storage

DreemFolio AI utilizes client-side localStorage solely for essential operational functionality:

  • saas_token: Authenticates your user session via signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
  • dreemfolio_theme: Remembers your Dark / Light display mode preference.
  • dreemfolio_country: Remembers your currency preference (LKR / USD).

We do not deploy intrusive third-party cross-site advertising trackers or tracking pixels.
