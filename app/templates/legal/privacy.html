Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026 • Your career data belongs to you. Here is our transparent disclosure on how your information is safeguarded.

Zero AI Training Retention

Your uploaded resumes and job descriptions are never used to train public foundation models.

256-Bit TLS & At-Rest Encryption

All data in transit is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3. User passwords are salted with bcrypt.

We Never Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal career information or contact lists to recruiters or advertisers.

1 Introduction & Data Controller

DreemFolio AI ("DreemFolio", "we", "our", or "us") provides a software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform enabling candidates and professionals to optimize resumes, generate ATS-friendly PDFs, draft cover letters, and host interactive web portfolios.

Under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation / GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), DreemFolio AI acts as the Data Controller for your account credentials and the Data Processor for the resumes and professional materials you process through our platform.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your legal data rights, contact our Data Protection Office at: privacy@dreemfolio.com.

2 Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: When creating an account, we collect your email address, full name, and an encrypted hash of your password (hashed via one-way bcrypt with random salt).
  • Google OAuth 2.0 Profile Information: If you choose to authenticate via "Continue with Google", we receive your Google ID, verified email address, full name, and profile picture avatar URL. We request only basic profile scopes (openid, email, profile).
  • Resume & Career Documents: Uploaded resumes (PDF, DOCX, TXT), employment history, education, skills, and pasted target job descriptions for keyword scoring.
  • Billing & Transaction Records: Subscription plan status (Free, Pro, Elite, Sprint Pass), expiry dates, and country/currency selection (LKR, USD). All credit card information is processed directly by PCI-DSS certified payment processors; DreemFolio never stores or sees your raw credit card numbers.
  • Technical Device Data: IP address, browser type, and referrer policy headers solely for fraud prevention, rate limiting, and brute-force mitigation.

3 Google OAuth & User Data Disclosure

DreemFolio AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We only access your Google data to authenticate your identity and populate your profile avatar and full name.
  • We do not transfer or disclose your Google user data to third parties, data brokers, or information resellers.
  • We never use Google user data to serve targeted advertisements.
  • We do not use Google user data for training generalized AI/ML models.

4 AI Generation & Zero-Training Guarantee

DreemFolio AI utilizes enterprise AI models (such as Google Gemini APIs) to analyze resume keywords, calculate ATS compatibility scores, tailor bullet points, and draft cover letters.

Zero Data Retention for Foundation Model Training

All data transmitted to AI inference endpoints is processed ephemerally over encrypted channels. We have strict agreements and enterprise flags configured so that your resume content, personal identifiers, and job descriptions are NEVER used by AI providers to train, fine-tune, or improve general AI models.

5 Data Retention & Cloud Storage

- Saved Cloud Resumes: Authenticated users who save resumes to MySQL Cloud retain their records until explicitly deleted by the user or upon account closure.
- Transient Uploads: Original uploaded files (raw DOCX or PDF parsing buffers) are parsed in memory and automatically cleared once structured data extraction is complete.
- Temporary Live Portfolios: Generated portfolio previews are cached in secure memory and deleted automatically if not published.

6 Your Legal Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of your geographic location, we provide all DreemFolio users with comprehensive data rights:

Right to Access:

You may request a machine-readable JSON copy of all resumes and account data stored in our database.

Right to Erasure (Be Forgotten):

You can request immediate and permanent deletion of your account and all associated cloud resumes at any time.

Right to Rectification:

You can edit or update any personal or career data in your profile via our web dashboard.

Right to Opt-Out / Non-Discrimination:

We never discriminate against users who exercise their privacy rights or request data removal.

7 Cookies & Local Browser Storage

DreemFolio AI uses client-side localStorage strictly for necessary operational functionality:

  • saas_token: Cryptographically signed JWT token to keep you authenticated across sessions.
  • dreemfolio_theme: Remembers your Dark / Light mode preference.
  • dreemfolio_country: Remembers your currency preference (LKR / USD).

We do not deploy intrusive cross-site behavioral tracking cookies.
