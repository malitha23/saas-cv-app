1. Applicant Tracking System (ATS) එකක් යනු කුමක්ද?

Applicant Tracking System (ATS) යනු Fortune 500 සමාගම් වලින් 99%ක් සහ අන්තර්ජාතික Startups බහුතරයක් (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Taleo) විසින් භාවිතා කරනු ලබන ස්වයංක්‍රීය මෘදුකාංග පද්ධතියකි. ඔබ රැකියාවකට CV එකක් Upload කළ විට, මුලින්ම එය කියවන්නේ මිනිස් Recruiter කෙනෙකු නොව මෙම ATS පද්ධතියයි.

ATS එක මඟින් ඔබගේ CV එකෙහි ඇති Contact Information, Work Experience, Education, Technical Skills වැනි තොරතුරු දත්ත පද්ධතියකට Extract කරගනී. ඔබගේ CV එකෙහි Layout එක සංකීර්ණ නම් හෝ Job Description එකෙහි ඇති Keywords නිවැරදිව නැතිනම්, ඔබ කොතරම් සුදුසුකම් සහිත අයෙකු වුවද CV එක ස්වයංක්‍රීයවම Reject වේ.

රීතිය 1: Single-Column Layout එකක් පමණක් භාවිතා කරන්න

Canva හෝ Figma වැනි මෘදුකාංග වලින් සාදන Two-column (තීරු දෙකේ) CV බැලූ බැල්මට ලස්සන වුවද, ATS Scanner එකක් ලේඛනයක් කියවන්නේ තිරස් අතට වමේ සිට දකුණටයි. තීරු දෙකක් ඇති විට වම් තීරුවේ ඇති Skills සහ දකුණු තීරුවේ ඇති Experience එකට මිශ්‍ර වී අර්ථ විරහිත වචන බවට පත්වේ. රීතිය 2: Tables, Text Boxes සහ Floating Graphics ඉවත් කරන්න

බොහෝ දෙනෙකු Contact info හෝ Skills දැමීමට Word/HTML Tables හෝ Text Boxes භාවිතා කරයි. නූතන ATS බොහෝමයක් Text Box හෝ Table එකක් තුළ ඇති දත්ත නොසලකා හරිමින් Drop කර දමයි. එබැවින් සියලුම දත්ත සාමාන්‍ය Page Flow එක තුළ තබන්න.

රීතිය 3: සම්මත Section Titles පමණක් භාවිතා කරන්න

ፈጠරාත්මක වචන වෙනුවට ATS පද්ධති පහසුවෙන් හඳුනාගන්නා සම්මත ශීර්ෂ පාඨ භාවිතා කරන්න:

  • Work Experience හෝ Professional Experience (යොදන්න එපා: My Career Story)
  • Technical Skills හෝ Core Competencies (යොදන්න එපා: Things I Love Doing)
  • Education (යොදන්න එපා: Academic Odyssey)

රීතිය 4: සම්මත Fonts සහ Margins

Inter, Roboto, Arial, Calibri, හෝ Helvetica වැනි විශ්වීය Unicode fonts භාවිතා කරන්න. Margin එක 0.5 inches සිට 0.75 inches අතර තබාගන්න.

රීතිය 5: PDF vs. DOCX — 2026 දී හොඳම කුමක්ද?

අතීතයේදී Word (.docx) වඩාත් සුදුසු යැයි පැවසුවද, 2026 වන විට Selectable Text සහිත Vector PDF වඩාත්ම සුදුසු Format එකයි. PDF මඟින් Mac, Windows, Linux ඕනෑම පරිගණකයක Layout එක සහ Fonts වෙනස් නොවී සුරක්ෂිතව තබා දෙයි.

3. Job Description එකට අනුව Keywords ගලපන ආකාරය

Recruiters ලා ATS එක තුළ Search කරන්නේ Boolean Queries මඟිනි (උදා: ("Python" OR "Golang") AND "Docker" AND "AWS"). ඔබගේ CV එකේ අදාළ Tools සහ Frameworks නියමිත අකුරින්ම සඳහන් නොවුනහොත් ඔබේ profile එක ඔවුන්ගේ සෙවුම් ලැයිස්තුවේ නොපෙනේ.

  • Full Forms සහ Acronyms දෙකම දක්වන්න: උදා: Amazon Web Services (AWS), Search Engine Optimization (SEO).
  • White Text Hacks කිසිදා භාවිත නොකරන්න: සුදු පැහැයෙන් Keywords සඟවා තැබීම නූතන AI ATS මඟින් පහසුවෙන්ම හඳුනාගෙන, වංචනික ක්‍රියාවක් ලෙස සලකා ඔබගේ අයදුම්පත ස්වයංක්‍රීයවම Blacklist කරනු ඇත.

4. Google XYZ Formula එක මඟින් Bullet Points ලිවීම

Recruiter කෙනෙකුගේ අවධානය දිනාගැනීමට සාමාන්‍ය රාජකාරී ලැයිස්තුගත නොකර Google සමාගම විසින් නිර්දේශ කරන XYZ Formula එක භාවිත කරන්න:

"Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]."

දුර්වල වාක්‍යයක්: Database speed වැඩි කර backend code එක ලිවීම.
ප්‍රබල ATS වාක්‍යයක්: Optimized PostgreSQL query indexing across 14 microservices [Z], reducing 99th-percentile API response latency by 42% [Y] and supporting 1.2M daily active users [X].

1. Applicant Tracking System (ATS) என்றால் என்ன?

Applicant Tracking System (ATS) என்பது உலகின் முன்னணி நிறுவனங்களால் (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Taleo) பயன்படுத்தப்படும் தானியங்கி மென்பொருளாகும். ஒரு வேலைக்கு உங்கள் CV ஐ சமர்ப்பிக்கும் போது, முதலில் அதை மனித ஆட்சேர்ப்பாளர் வாசிப்பதில்லை; இந்த ATS மென்பொருளே பரிசீலிக்கிறது.

உங்கள் CV இல் உள்ள Contact Details, Work Experience, Education மற்றும் Skills ஆகியவற்றை ATS தரவுத்தளத்தில் பிரித்தெடுக்கிறது. உங்கள் CV இன் கட்டமைப்பு குழப்பமாக இருந்தாலோ அல்லது தேவையான முக்கிய வார்த்தைகள் இல்லையென்றாலோ, உங்கள் விண்ணப்பம் உடனடியாக நிராகரிக்கப்படும்.

1. What Is an Applicant Tracking System (ATS)?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is enterprise software utilized by 99% of Fortune 500 corporations and over 75% of fast-growing startups—including tools like Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Taleo, and iCIMS. When you upload your CV through a company careers portal or job board, the ATS acts as an automated first-line gatekeeper.

Rather than presenting your beautifully styled layout to a human recruiter right away, the ATS extracts your text into database fields: Contact Information, Work Experience, Education, Technical Skills, and Certifications. If the parser cannot understand your resume geometry, or if your document lacks the specific keywords matching the recruiter's search filter, your application is automatically ranked at the bottom of the candidate stack or quietly rejected without human eyes ever glancing at it.

2. The 5 Non-Negotiable Rules of ATS Formatting

To ensure 100% optical character recognition (OCR) and accurate database mapping, your resume must strictly obey these structural principles:

Rule #1: Enforce a Clean, Single-Column Layout

While two-column templates with colorful left sidebars look aesthetically pleasing in graphic design software like Canva or Figma, ATS parsers read documents horizontally from left to right. When an ATS encounters two columns, it reads across columns in a single line, turning your job titles, dates, and bullet points into an unintelligible word salad. DreemFolio AI generates strictly verified single-column vector geometry that guarantees zero parsing collisions.

Rule #2: Eliminate Tables, Text Boxes, and Floating Graphics

Many job seekers place their contact information or skills inside invisible HTML/Word tables or floating graphical text boxes. Modern ATS scanners frequently drop all content enclosed inside text boxes or floating frames. Keep all text in the standard body flow.

Rule #3: Use Standard, Universal Headings

Do not try to be overly creative with section titles. An ATS uses natural language algorithms programmed to look for specific standard headings:

  • Use: Work Experience or Professional Experience (Avoid: My Career Voyage)
  • Use: Technical Skills or Core Competencies (Avoid: Things I Am Great At)
  • Use: Education (Avoid: Academic Credentials)

Rule #4: Standard Typography and Safe Margins

Use modern, clean fonts with universal unicode glyphs such as Inter, Roboto, Arial, Calibri, or Helvetica. Avoid obscure custom web fonts or icon-based bullet symbols that decode as missing character squares. Maintain margins between 0.5 inches and 0.75 inches to ensure standard page boundaries.

Rule #5: File Format: PDF vs. DOCX in 2026

In 2026, standard vector PDF is the gold standard, provided it is generated with selectable text rather than flattened raster images. PDFs preserve exact layout and typography across every recruiter's operating system (Mac, Windows, Linux).

3. How to Optimize Keywords Without "Keyword Stuffing"

Recruiters search ATS databases using Boolean queries such as: ("Python" OR "Golang") AND ("Kubernetes" OR "Docker") AND "AWS". If your resume uses different acronyms or omits the exact tool names, your profile will never appear in their search filter.

  • Include Both Full Terms and Common Acronyms: Write out Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Amazon Web Services (AWS), or Continuous Integration / Continuous Deployment (CI/CD).
  • Never Resort to "White Text" Hacks: In 2026, modern ATS algorithms automatically convert all text to raw plain-text ASCII during extraction. White text is instantly exposed, triggering an automated red flag and disqualification for fraud.

4. The Google XYZ Formula for High-Impact Bullet Points

Passing the ATS is step one; persuading the human recruiter who opens your resume is step two. Avoid listing mundane daily responsibilities. Instead, adopt the battle-tested Google XYZ formula:

"Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]."
